One account

Pick an account from any list, or type its id above. The map shows the money around it: who paid it on the left, who it paid on the right. Every arrow points the way the money moved.

04

Structures

A ranked account is a name, and an investigator cannot act on a name. These are groups of accounts that only make sense read together, ranked by what an investigator can act on. Each carries its breaking point: the one account whose removal breaks most of it.

05

Replay the month

July 2026, one day at a time, on the same hop layout: known clients on the left, four hops to the right. Watch which days carry the bursts and how far the money travels in a day.

06

Amount echoes

An exact sum arrives at an account and leaves again as the same sum, or split into parts, within a day. Or one account sends the identical amount to several receivers on the same day. Each is a hypothesis for an analyst to check against what the transfers were said to be for.
